Welcome to ATHARRYS PROPERTIES LIMITED

Your Trusted Real Estate Partner Since 2019

ATHARRYS PROPERTIES LIMITED was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to transform the real estate experience in Nigeria by providing exceptional service, transparency, and expertise to every client we serve.

We are a trustworthy partner for your property needs. We provide the best-in-class property management services for owners and investors alike while providing hands-on leasing support and assistance throughout the life cycle of any given project. Furnished with a team that is rich in experience, we have consistently reached milestones that have earned us a successful track record with investments around Nigeria and beyond.

At ATHARRYS PROPERTIES, we provide you with the best and most valuable real estate experiences. We offer a unique opportunity to diversify your portfolio and increase the value of your assets through investment in carefully selected properties in Nigeria and beyond.

Meet Our Founder

The visionary leader behind ATHARRYS PROPERTIES LIMITED

Mr Harry Emokai

Founder & CEO

With over 20 years of experience in real estate, Mr. Emokai founded ATHARRYS PROPERTIES in 2019 with a simple yet powerful vision: to transform the real estate experience in Nigeria by providing exceptional service, transparency, and expertise to every client.

Under his leadership, ATHARRYS has grown from a small startup to one of Nigeria's most trusted real estate companies, successfully handling over 500 property transactions and serving more than 1200 satisfied clients across Lagos.
